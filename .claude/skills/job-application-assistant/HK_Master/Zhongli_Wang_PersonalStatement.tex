\noindent
\textbf{Application for Master of Science in Data Science and Analytics}

\vspace{0.1cm}

\noindent Dear Admissions Committee,

I have developed a strong and sustained interest in data science throughout my academic journey, and I am eager to deepen my expertise in this field through rigorous, specialized training in Hong Kong.
My interest in data science began during my undergraduate studies at Nankai University, where I pursued a BSc in Finance alongside a BA in German Language and Literature. During this time, I recognized that extracting meaningful insights from financial data required not just domain knowledge but also sophisticated analytical techniques. This realization led me to pursue a Master's degree in Business and Economics at the University of Zurich, where I deliberately chose a minor in Data Science to complement my major in Banking and Finance. My thesis, ``An Approach to Identify the Financial Statement Frauds in the China Concepts Stock Market Using Financial Metrics and Machine Learning Methods,'' demonstrated my commitment to applying machine learning and statistical techniques to real-world business problems.

Throughout my doctoral research at Bielefeld University and Università Cattolica del Sacro Cuore, I have continued to develop and apply advanced data science skills. My work has involved developing computational models, conducting large-scale data analysis, and applying machine learning techniques (XGBoost, Random Forest, Neural Networks) to extract insights from complex datasets. I have published research on financial fraud detection using supervised learning methods, and I have collaborated on projects involving agentic workflows and AI-powered data analysis systems. 
% These experiences have reinforced my conviction that data science—combining mathematics, statistics, and computer science—is essential for solving real-world problems across finance, technology, and business.

I am particularly drawn to your program because of its balanced treatment of the three pillars of modern data science: mathematics, statistics, and computer science. The curriculum, with its combination of compulsory subjects in big data computing, statistical data mining, optimization and deep learning, aligns perfectly with my goal of developing comprehensive expertise in data science and analytics. 
I am especially interested in electives such as Advanced Data Analytics, Natural Language Processing, and Artificial Intelligence applications, 
which will enable me to specialize in areas relevant to my career aspirations in data-driven business and technology sectors.

Studying in Hong Kong is particularly important to me because of its position as a global financial and technology hub. 
I believe that pursuing this degree in Hong Kong will not only provide me with world-class education but also expose me to diverse industry applications of data science, networking opportunities with leading practitioners, and the chance to develop my career in one of the world's most dynamic data science markets.

My strong foundation in economics, finance, and quantitative methods, combined with my demonstrated commitment to data science throughout my studies, makes me a well-prepared candidate for this program. 
I am eager to contribute meaningfully to the cohort and to apply the knowledge and skills I gain to advance data science and analytics in my future career. 

Thank you so much for considering my application!
