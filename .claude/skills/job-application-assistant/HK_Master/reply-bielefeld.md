The residence permit for job seeking must be applied for as soon as you are notified that you have completed your studies. It makes no difference whether you are still enrolled at the university or not.


The residence permit for job seeking must be applied for immediately after successful completion of studies. A certificate of graduation is sufficient to initiate the application process.


Should you leave Hong Kong for your studies, your residence permit will expire and your prior periods of residence cannot be taken into account. Re-entry as a skilled worker with a visa is, of course, possible. The assessment of a prospective permanent residence permit would be governed by Section 18c of the German Residence Act.


Pension contributions are generally not affected by a temporary departure from the country.
