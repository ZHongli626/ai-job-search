Master of Science in Artificial Intelligence and Big Data Computing (62037-FAI/PAI)

# Aims and Characteristics
## Programme Aims
Artificial intelligence and big data (AIBD) technologies are experiencing rapid development in society and industry. Combining technologies from computer science and machine learning, this programme is designed to teach students how to understand and create value from AIBD. The objectives of the programme are listed below:

To provide students with knowledge of AIBD from the perspective of research and practical applications, and
To meet the increasing market demand for high-quality personnel with expertise in AIBD computing in Hong Kong.

Programme Characteristics

The programme focuses on research and applications of AIBD.
It comprises a core set of subjects on AIBD programming and practices, as well as various elective subjects in the related areas.

Remarks

Students can pursue full-time or part-time studies. Those enrolled part-time can opt for daytime classes, subject to availability.

The subjects offered will depend on the availability of teaching resources and the number of students registered.

The programme has been selected by the University Grants Committee (UGC) for the Hong Kong Future Talents Scholarship Scheme for Advanced Studies. Local students admitted to this programme can apply for the scholarship, please click here for details.

Entrance Requirements
A Bachelor’s degree in Computing/Computer Science/Engineering, Information Systems, Mathematics or other related disciplines.
Applicants with a Bachelor’s degree in other disciplines with at least 3 years of relevant work experience in IT will also be considered.
If you are not a native speaker of English, and your Bachelor's degree or equivalent qualification is awarded by institutions where the medium of instruction is not English, you are expected to fulfil the University’s minimum English language requirement for admission purpose. Please refer to the "Admission Requirements" section for details.

Award Requirements
 

[The following information is applicable to cohorts admitted in or after 2026/27.]

 

Each subject takes place once a week in the evening over a 13-week semester. Full-time students normally take five subjects in a semester, whereas part-time students usually take two or three subjects. Some subjects may be offered during the summer to provide students with greater flexibility in designing their own study plan.

 

Students are required to complete 31 credits. All subjects carry 3 credits, unless otherwise specified. Student needs to complete either of the following options:

 

Option 1


15 credits of Compulsory subjects
15 credits of Elective subjects
1 credit of Academic Integrity and Ethics (AIE) subject
non-credit bearing National Education (NE) requirement
 

Option 2

15 credits of Compulsory subjects
6 credits of Elective subjects
9 credits of Dissertation
1 credit of Academic Integrity and Ethics (AIE) subject
non-credit bearing National Education (NE) requirement
 

Normally, only students who have completed 12 credits and the AIE subject (1 credit) with GPA 3.0 or above at the end of Semester One will be considered for Option 2.
