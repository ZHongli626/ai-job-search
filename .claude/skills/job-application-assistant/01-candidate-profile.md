## Research Projects

- **Strategic Firm Capacity Selection under Network Effects** (Job Market Paper): Agent-based model of competing ICT firms strategically adjusting R&D investments in service capacity. Analyzes how network effects and capacity constraints shape profitability and consumer behavior in digital markets.

- **Polytope Fraud Theory** (with Zhao, Schweizer, Sornette): Published in *International Review of Financial Analysis* (2025). Introduced ten financial red flags from activist short sellers to enhance fraud detection using XGBoost and supervised learning.

- **Agentic Workflows for Economic Research** (with Wang, Yi, Dawid, Harting): Framework combining LLMs and multimodal AI to automate economic research tasks. Implemented using Microsoft's AutoGen.

- **Heterogeneous Consumers and the Data Value Chain**: Hybrid game-theoretic and agent-based model investigating how data restriction affects geographical dynamics of software markets.

- **Parameter Estimation Methods in Agent-based Computational Models** (Work in Progress): Calibrated model of ICT industry comparing simulated method of moments and neural network surrogates using AT&T and T-Mobile financial data.
