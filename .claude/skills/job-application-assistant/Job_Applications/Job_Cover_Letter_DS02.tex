\noindent Dear Recruiting Team,

I am currently completing my Ph.D.\ in Economics at Bielefeld University with planned completion in September 2026. My doctoral research combines data analysis, statistical modeling, machine learning, simulation, and business-oriented interpretation of complex economic systems. I am very interested in the position because it offers the opportunity to apply data science to real business challenges in insurance, pricing, risk management, and evidence-based decision-making.

During my doctoral studies, I have developed analytical workflows for complex economic and business questions. This includes data collection, cleaning, validation, feature engineering, robustness checks, and the communication of quantitative results. In one dissertation project, I estimate an agent-based model of competition and investment dynamics using financial and operational data from AT\&T and T-Mobile, applying simulation-based estimation. In another project, I use supervised machine-learning methods to detect financial fraud in the U.S.\ market, working with a broad set of financial indicators and evaluating model performance through predictive accuracy and feature importance. I can transform complex datasets into reliable insights and connect technical analysis with practical business interpretation.

My technical background is closely aligned with QBE's requirements for this role. I have strong experience in Python, including pandas, NumPy, scikit-learn, XGBoost, statistical analysis, machine learning and simulations. I am also familiar with R and have working knowledge of SQL for analytical querying, joins, aggregation, and data validation. I have experience working in Linux-based research environments and using reproducible workflows for large-scale modeling and simulation tasks. I am currently strengthening my practical skills in Power BI and MLOps-related workflows, including version control, model documentation, and the translation of analytical outputs into business-facing reporting tools.

Beyond technical modeling, I value clear communication and collaboration with non-technical stakeholders. My academic work has required me to present complex quantitative findings through papers, reports, presentations, and visualisations for different audiences. I would be excited to work closely with analytics leads, business partners, and internal stakeholders at QBE to understand their needs, define suitable analytical approaches, and deliver clear recommendations that can influence action. QBE focuses on becoming a consistent and innovative risk partner. I am motivated by the opportunity to apply data science in an insurance environment where pricing, risk assessment, customer outcomes, and business improvement are closely connected. I would be grateful for the opportunity to contribute my strengths in machine learning, statistical modeling, and structured problem solving while continuing to grow as a data scientist in Hong Kong.

Thank you so much for considering my application! I would like to discuss how I can contribute to QBE's Data \& Analytics team in Asia.
