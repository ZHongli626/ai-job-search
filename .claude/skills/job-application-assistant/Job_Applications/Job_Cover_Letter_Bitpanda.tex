\noindent Dear Recruiting Team,

I am currently completing my Ph.D.\ in Economics at Bielefeld University and Universit\`a Cattolica del Sacro Cuore, with planned completion in September 2026. My research combines quantitative economic modelling, financial and operational data analysis, simulation, and risk-oriented empirical methods. I am very interested in the Associate, Treasury position at Bitpanda because it offers the opportunity to apply my analytical and programming skills to Asset and Liability Management, reconciliation, risk analysis, financial reporting, and the evolving market for digital assets.

Across my doctoral research, I have developed end-to-end analytical workflows for complex economic and business systems. This includes data collection, cleaning, validation, feature engineering, model construction, simulation, robustness checks, and the synthesis of quantitative findings into structured reports. In one dissertation project, I estimate an agent-based model of competition and investment dynamics using financial and operational data from AT\&T and T-Mobile, applying simulation-based estimation and Python-based analytical workflows. This experience has strengthened my ability to work carefully with complex datasets, assess data consistency, evaluate assumptions, and translate quantitative outputs into decision-relevant insights.

My background is particularly relevant to the Treasury Associate role because much of my work has focused on financial and strategic decision-making under uncertainty. I have studied how firms manage investment, capacity, profitability, and market risk in dynamic environments, and I have also conducted research on financial fraud detection using machine-learning methods. These projects have trained me to approach data with rigorous attention to detail, to identify irregular patterns, and to communicate complex financial and statistical concepts clearly to different audiences.

I am confident that I can contribute to Bitpanda's Treasury function in areas such as ALM reconciliation, reporting, liquidity and market risk monitoring, and the continuous improvement of analytical tools. My strongest technical skills are in Python-based data analysis, including pandas, NumPy, statistical modelling, machine learning, and reproducible research pipelines. I also use SQL for analytical querying, joins, aggregation, and data validation, and I am actively strengthening my SQL skills further, especially for reconciliation-style queries, CTEs, window functions, and finance-data workflows. I would be highly motivated to apply and deepen these skills in a fast-paced fintech environment.

Bitpanda is especially attractive to me because of its role at the intersection of finance, technology, and digital assets. I am keen to develop practical expertise in Treasury operations, ALM, liquidity risk, and crypto asset-liability monitoring while contributing my strengths in quantitative analysis, structured problem solving, and automation. I would be excited to learn from experienced Treasury professionals and to support reporting structures and analytical tools that improve data integrity, risk visibility, and business decision-making.

Thank you very much for considering my application. I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my background in economics, quantitative modelling, Python-based analytics, and financial risk research can contribute to Bitpanda's Treasury team.
