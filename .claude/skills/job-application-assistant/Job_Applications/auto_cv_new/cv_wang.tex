\section{Research}

\textbullet \;\textbf{Strategic Firm Capacity Selection under the Network Effects: A Computational Model of Capacity Investment Dynamics in the ICT Industry} (Job Market Paper) [\href{https://drive.google.com/file/d/1wa6D0VgsMyzZHyDrStmR7gF94wcbUio_/view?usp=sharing}{Link}] \\ [1.5ex]
\textcolor{BlueViolet}{This paper develops an agent-based model of competing ICT firms that strategically adjust their physical R\&D investments in service capacity, rather than innovation, to manage congestion and meet consumer demand. It highlights how network effects and capacity constraints jointly shape profitability, product quality, and consumer behavior in digital markets.}

\textbullet\ \textbf{Polytope Fraud Theory} (with Dongshuai Zhao, Florian Schweizer, Didier Sornette) \textit{International Review of Financial Analysis}, Volume 97, 2025. [\href{https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1057521924006665?via%3Dihub}{Link}] \\ [1.5ex]
\textcolor{BlueViolet}{This paper introduces ten financial red flags derived from the behavior of activist short sellers to enhance fraud detection. Using a manually labeled dataset and supervised learning (notably XGBoost), we show these signals can reliably identify fraudulent firms based on public financial data.}
% I contributed to the development of the Polytope Fraud Theory by collecting and labeling data on activist short sellers' targets, designing financial features, and implementing machine learning models—especially XGBoost—to detect financial statement fraud using publicly available accounting data.

\textbullet\ \textbf{Agentic Workflows for Economic Research: Design and Implementation} (with Hankui Wang, Jiachen Yi, Herbert Dawid, Philipp Harting) [\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.09736}{arXiv}]\\ [1.5ex]
\textcolor{BlueViolet}{This paper proposes an agentic workflow framework for economic research, combining Large Language Models and multimodal AI to automate tasks across the research lifecycle—from literature review to empirical analysis—with human-in-the-loop oversight. Implemented using Microsoft’s AutoGen, the framework demonstrates how specialized AI agents can enhance efficiency, reproducibility, and methodological rigor in economic research.}

\textbullet\ \textbf{Heterogeneous Consumers and the Data Value Chain: An Agent-Based Approach} [\href{https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=5235196}{SSRN}]\\ [1.5ex]
\textcolor{BlueViolet}{This paper investigates how varying levels of data restriction affect the geographical dynamics of software markets using a hybrid game-theoretic and agent-based model. The results show that stricter data regulations influence price formation and market structure, highlighting the need for coordinated policy to balance consumer welfare and digital industry development.}

\textbullet \;\textbf{Parameter Estimation Methods in an Agent-based Computational Model: Insights on ICT Industrial Dynamics and Policies} (in progress)\\ [1.5ex]
\textcolor{BlueViolet}{This paper addresses the challenge of parameter estimation in agent-based modeling by developing a calibrated model of the ICT industry, where firm strategies and network effects matter. Using financial data from AT\&T and T-Mobile, we compare simulated method of moments and neural network surrogates to assess their effectiveness and enhance the empirical reliability of ABMs in applied economic research.}
