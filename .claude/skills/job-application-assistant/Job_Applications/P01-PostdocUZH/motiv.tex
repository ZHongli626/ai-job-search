\opening{Dear Professor Keil,}

I am writing to express my interest in the postdoctoral position at the Chair of International Management. As I approach the completion of my PhD in Economics at Bielefeld University, I am eager to continue my academic career in a research environment that aligns closely with my expertise in business analysis, simulation-based modeling, and applied quantitative methods.

My doctoral thesis, titled \textit{Strategic Behavior and Economic Dynamics in ICT Markets: An Agent-Based Framework}, investigates firm competition, price and investment strategies within dynamic market environments. The work employs agent-based simulations and quantitative analysis to study complex strategic interactions and technological evolution in the ICT sector. This background has equipped me with strong computational skills and a solid foundation in business modeling that I believe are well-suited to the focus of your research group.

What attracts me particularly to the Chair of International Management is its strong emphasis on strategic management challenges faced by multinational enterprises and its commitment to producing research that is both theoretically rigorous and practically relevant. I am especially drawn to the Chair’s focus on long-term organizational performance, growth, and strategic renewal—areas that resonate with my own work on firm dynamics, investment strategies, and simulation-based modeling. I see meaningful synergies between my methodological background and the Chair’s use of quantitative and simulation approaches to address real-world managerial problems. At the current stage, I remain fully committed to finalizing and submitting my doctoral thesis through my own efforts within the expected timeline.

I would be honored to join your team, collaborate on international publications, and contribute to research activities aligned with the chair’s strategic focus. Please find my CV and supporting materials enclosed. I would be happy to provide additional information or references upon request. 

Thank you so much for considering my application! I look forward to the opportunity to discuss how my background and research interests may contribute to your ongoing work.
