\noindent Dear Hiring Manager,

I am writing to apply for the Technology Research Analyst position in your Research Department. I am currently completing the final stage of my Ph.D. in Economics at Bielefeld University, with my dissertation defense scheduled for September 21, 2026, and I also hold an M.A. in Business and Economics with a Banking and Finance major and a B.Sc. in Finance. My background combines financial analysis, technology-market research, quantitative modeling, and research communication in English and Mandarin Chinese.

My closest fit with this role is research that connects company fundamentals with technology-market structure. In my job market paper, I model competing ICT firms' R\&D capacity choices under network effects and analyze how capacity constraints shape profitability and consumer behavior. In related work, I study the data value chain in software markets and calibrate industry models using AT\&T and T-Mobile financial data. These projects have trained me to break a sector into value drivers, test assumptions, and translate complex evidence into clear written arguments.

I would bring the following strengths to your fundamental research team:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Industry and company analysis:} Experience analyzing ICT competition, network effects, data-driven business models, and firm-level strategic decisions.
    \item \textbf{Financial research discipline:} A published financial-statement fraud detection project using accounting, financial, and market indicators with XGBoost to identify red flags in listed companies.
    \item \textbf{Model-building toolkit:} Advanced Python skills, plus R, Julia, Mathematica, econometrics, machine learning, and scenario-based computational modeling that can support industry, company, and valuation work.
    \item \textbf{Communication and collaboration:} Academic papers, conference presentations, teaching support in Corporate Finance, and international research collaboration across China and Europe, supported by fluent Mandarin Chinese and English.
\end{itemize}

I am particularly interested in this opportunity because it combines bottom-up company research, global technology markets, and close collaboration with portfolio managers. I would aim to support investment decisions by building well-documented models, challenging assumptions, gathering insights from company management, sell-side analysts, and industry experts, and turning complex evidence into concise research reports. My long-term goal is to develop as an investor in technology, and this analyst role offers the right environment to build that craft through rigorous coverage work.

Thank you for your time and consideration. I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my research background, financial training, and technology-sector analytical work can contribute to your Research Department.
