\noindent \textbf{Secondment Partner:} Lucidminds AI (Netherlands)\\
\textbf{Linked Project:} \emph{AI, Networks, and the Diffusion of Digital Technologies in Nordic Societies}\\[0.4cm]

\section*{Rationale and Fit}

Lucidminds AI is an applied research organisation working at the intersection of systems modelling, behavioural insights, and AI-supported decision tools. Its expertise in diffusion modelling and inequality-sensitive design aligns directly with the fellowship’s investigation of how digital and AI technologies spread through Nordic societies.  
The applicant previously completed an internship with Lucidminds, ensuring rapid onboarding, established communication channels, and strong willingness from the organisation to host the secondment.

\section*{Objectives}

\begin{itemize}
\item Strengthen agent-based and network-diffusion modelling central to the project.
\item Translate academic models into transparent, stakeholder-oriented tools.
\item Integrate behavioural and distributional considerations into scenario design.
\item Use LLM-assisted workflows to analyse adoption barriers in unstructured text.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Planned Activities}

\begin{itemize}
\item Build a modular simulation environment with calibration inputs and robustness checks.
\item Develop explainable behavioural rules (peer effects, frictions, heterogeneous costs).
\item Implement reproducible LLM pipelines to extract narratives from Nordic media and policy texts.
\item Co-design lightweight prototypes or dashboards communicating policy-relevant scenarios.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Supervision and Collaboration}

Lucidminds will provide regular supervision from senior systems designers and data scientists, guidance on scalable model architectures, and iterative feedback to ensure practical relevance, clarity, and methodological soundness.

\section*{Knowledge Transfer and Added Value}

The secondment enables two-way knowledge transfer:  
(1) academic rigour in modelling diffusion under network interactions;  
(2) industry practices for building usable tools and communicating uncertainty to stakeholders.  
This strengthens the intersectoral dimension of the fellowship and supports Finland’s national priorities in AI and digitalisation.

Expected Deliverables include: A documented simulation module for AI-diffusion scenarios, a prototype or specification for an interactive policy-scenario tool, and a short technical note detailing modelling choices, validation, and reproducibility.
