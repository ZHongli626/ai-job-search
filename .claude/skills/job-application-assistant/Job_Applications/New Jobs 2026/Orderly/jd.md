About the job
Work with the best

We are Orderly, the ultimate destination for decentralized trading platforms. We are not satisfied with the limitations of centralized exchanges or the complexities of building decentralized order books. We aim to make trading easy, fast, and secure for everyone. How? By fusing the simplicity of CeFi with the power of DeFi.

At the heart of the issue lies “liquidity”. Builders often grapple with a paradox; traders need liquidity, but liquidity demands traders. Our solution is simple as is bold –concentrate all liquidity into one order book. One order book that connects all the liquidity in the crypto space.

Our ambition doesn't stop there. We're omnichain, championing chain abstraction. We envision seamless transactions, where the underlying blockchain is merely a detail. By concentrating liquidity, we aim to redefine the crypto landscape, making bridging and wrapped assets a thing of the past.

We are Orderly, and we are here to revolutionize DeFi, democratizing access to financial opportunities and fostering a fair, equitable paradigm for all.

A Glimpse into Your Future at Orderly Network

What will you be working on?
Process, clean, and validate liquidity and trading data to ensure accuracy and usability.
Extract and transform trading data to generate regular reports (weekly/monthly) and support alerting mechanisms.
Monitor liquidity dashboards and identify anomalies in a timely manner.
Maintain and update market parameter configuration tables.
Support market maker onboarding and assist with incentive distribution processes.
Participate in data analysis for liquidity mechanism design and assist in testing new incentive models.
Who will you be working with?
Work closely with the liquidity operations team on daily monitoring and execution.
Collaborate with product, trading, and data teams to ensure data accuracy and operational efficiency.
Support coordination with market makers during onboarding and ongoing operations.
What challenges will you face?
Handling large volumes of trading data with high accuracy requirements.
Identifying anomalies and ensuring data quality in a fast-moving trading environment.
Balancing data processing, monitoring, and operational support tasks simultaneously.
Adapting quickly to evolving liquidity strategies and incentive mechanisms.
