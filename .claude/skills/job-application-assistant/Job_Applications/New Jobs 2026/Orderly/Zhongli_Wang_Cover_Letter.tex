\vspace{0.15cm}

\noindent Dear Orderly hiring team,

I am applying for the Data Analyst Intern position at Orderly. 
I am completing a Ph.D. in Economics at Bielefeld University, with my defense scheduled for September 21, 2026, 
and I hold an M.A. in Business and Economics with a major in Banking and Finance (minor in Data Science). 
My background includes programming, financial data analysis, statistical modeling, and careful validation of complex datasets, 
which I would bring to liquidity monitoring and trading-data operations.

Orderly's focus on shared liquidity and orderbook-based trading infrastructure makes data quality especially important. 
I would be motivated to support the liquidity operations team by cleaning and validating trading data, producing reliable weekly or monthly reports, 
monitoring dashboards for anomalies, and documenting changes to market-parameter tables. 
I would also bring a methodical approach to operational tasks that require accuracy while coordinating with product, trading, data, and market-maker teams.

My experience supports this contribution in concrete ways:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Analyze financial data:} In a published fraud-detection project, I worked with accounting, financial, and market indicators and applied XGBoost to identify potential red flags in listed companies.
    \item \textbf{Build and test models:} My doctoral research uses computational models, parameter estimation, and simulation to study strategic firm behavior, market structure, and outcomes under uncertainty.
    \item \textbf{Work reproducibly:} I use Python, R, Julia, Mathematica and SQL to process data, check assumptions, compare results, and communicate analytical findings clearly.
    \item \textbf{Learn unfamiliar domains quickly:} My finance training and research in data-driven markets give me a strong foundation for learning liquidity mechanisms, incentive models, and Web3 trading operations.
\end{itemize}

I would approach the role with both relevant analytical experience and a willingness to learn Orderly's systems and market processes carefully. 
I would welcome the opportunity to contribute reliable analysis and disciplined operational support while developing practical experience in decentralized trading infrastructure.

Thank you so much for your consideration! I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my quantitative background and data-analysis experience could support Orderly's liquidity operations.
