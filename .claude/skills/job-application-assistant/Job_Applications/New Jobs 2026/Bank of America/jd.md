Job Description – Regional Economist (Asia) & Korea Economist (Associate level):

As a Korea and regional Economist in the Asia economics team, you will be responsible for writing research and making forecasts on the regional economy within Asia, with a concentration in Korea economy. The research will focus on the macroeconomic environment and its implications for investment in equity, interest rate and foreign exchange markets.

You will work on the Korea, regional and global economics teams, and in close conjunction across the broader research team both domestically and globally. You will be involved in compiling and analyzing economic data, quantitative analysis, economic forecasting, ad hoc client requests, as well as making regular and ad-hoc contributions to written research, along with interactions with both internal and external clients. Over time, the successful candidate will be expected to develop independent views on the Korean and regional economies and take increasing ownership of economic forecasts, research publications, and client interactions. The position is located in Hong Kong.

 

Required Experience:

Master's or Ph.D. degree in Economics, Finance, or a related quantitative field
Preferably 3-5 years of relevant experience in macroeconomic research, economic policy, financial markets, or a related analytical role; fresh Ph.D. graduates are eligible to apply.
Strong understanding of macroeconomics with demonstrated interests in Korea and Asian economies, monetary and fiscal policy, and financial markets
Excellent written and verbal communication skills in English. Fluency in Korean is strongly preferred; proficiency in another major Asian language would be an advantage.
Strong quantitative and analytical skills, including experience in economic forecasting, econometric analysis, and large economic datasets
Proficiency in Excel and at least one programming/statistical language such as Python, R, Stata, MATLAB, or EViews. Experience with quantitative research and model development is a plus
Familiarity with economic and financial data platforms such as Bloomberg, CEIC, and Wind; experience with Korean statistical databases would be advantageous
Ability to communicate economic views clearly and effectively and to build strong relationships with internal and external clients
Demonstrated ability to manage multiple priorities under tight deadlines, with a high level of initiative, intellectual curiosity, accuracy, and attention to detail
Strong team player with the ability to collaborate effectively across countries, functions, and time zones.
