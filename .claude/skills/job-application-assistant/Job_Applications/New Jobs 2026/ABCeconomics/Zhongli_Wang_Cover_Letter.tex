\noindent Dear ABC economics recruiting team,

I have successfully completed my Ph.D. in Economics at Bielefeld University, with my defense on September 21, 2026, 
and I hold an M.A. in Business and Economics with a major in Banking and Finance and a minor in Data Science. 
My doctoral research focuses on computational economics and industrial organization, 
specifically analyzing strategic firm behavior, market structure, and competitive dynamics in ICT markets, 
providing a strong foundation for economic and econometric analysis of complex business and regulatory issues.

I am particularly drawn to ABC economics because of its reputation for providing independent economic and econometric advice on competition and regulatory matters, combining scientific knowledge with intellectual rigour and sector expertise. 
The opportunity to contribute to high-level domestic and international cases—from international arbitrations and merger reviews to antitrust proceedings and damage quantification—aligns perfectly with my research approach: 
develop theoretical frameworks, relate issues to relevant economic theory, and support analysis with solid and persuasive empirical evidence.
My relevant experience comprises:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Industrial organization and market analysis:} My doctoral research develops computational models to analyze strategic firm behavior, capacity investment decisions, market structure, and competitive dynamics in ICT markets. I study how network effects, service capacity, and strategic interactions shape market outcomes, which are directly relevant to competition and regulatory analysis.
    \item \textbf{Econometric and quantitative analysis:} I have extensive experience with econometric methods, parameter estimation, and statistical analysis using Stata, R, and Python. I have conducted empirical analysis on large-scale firm-level datasets, demonstrating proficiency in collecting, cleaning, and analyzing qualitative and quantitative data to understand cases and support arguments.
    \item \textbf{Economic modeling and theory:} I develop agent-based models to analyze firm strategies under uncertainty, estimate structural parameters, and test theoretical predictions against empirical data. I have authored peer-reviewed publications and prepared technical reports, with strong drafting skills and experience translating complex economic concepts to non-economists.
\end{itemize}

I have excellent command of both English and German at C1 level. 
I am highly motivated to apply my expertise in industrial organization, econometrics, and quantitative modeling to competition and regulatory economics. 
I am eager to contribute to ABC economics' high-profile cases, develop deep subject-matter expertise across different sectors, and work collaboratively with experienced colleagues. 
I am ready to engage with clients, authorities, and legal teams, and to continue developing my technical and communication skills in this dynamic field.

Thank you for considering my application! 
I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my background and abilities could contribute to ABC economics' competition and regulatory practice.
