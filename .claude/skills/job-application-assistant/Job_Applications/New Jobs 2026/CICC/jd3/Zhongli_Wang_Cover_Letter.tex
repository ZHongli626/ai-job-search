\textbf{Application for Risk Analyst}

\vspace{0.1cm}

\noindent Dear CICC Recruiting Team,

I have successfully completed my Ph.D. in Economics at Bielefeld University (defense: September 21, 2026), following an M.A. in Banking \& Finance from the University of Zurich and a double bachelor's degree in Finance and German Language and Literature from Nankai University. As a native Mandarin speaker with C1-level English, I combine rigorous quantitative training with published research experience in financial risk analysis. The Risk Analyst position at CICC's Risk Management Department is an ideal match for my background in risk measurement, financial modeling, and data-driven analysis.

My experience aligns directly with the responsibilities of this role:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Risk measurement and financial modeling:} My doctoral research develops computational and econometric models to analyze firm behavior and market outcomes under uncertainty, including parameter estimation and sensitivity analysis. I am proficient in building financial models in Python, with additional experience in R and SQL.
    \item \textbf{Financial statement analysis and fraud detection:} I have published peer-reviewed research applying machine learning methods (XGBoost, Random Forest, Neural Networks) to financial statement analysis and fraud detection, demonstrating strong ability to identify risk patterns in accounting metrics and market indicators.
    \item \textbf{Risk management and regulatory knowledge:} My internships at the People's Bank of China and the Agricultural Bank of China provided first-hand understanding of financial regulation, reserve requirements, banking operations, and internal risk and reporting systems (FMIS), building a solid foundation for operational and enterprise risk assessment.
    \item \textbf{Data analytics and reporting:} I am experienced in risk data analysis, data visualization, and preparing clear analytical reports for stakeholders. At LucidMinds (Amsterdam), I delivered scenario analyses and trade-off summaries to international business stakeholders in English, combining quantitative rigor with concise business writing in both English and Chinese.
\end{itemize}

I am particularly drawn to the breadth of CICC's Risk Management Department, spanning enterprise risk, credit risk, and market risk, as well as its digital transformation initiatives in risk data analysis and system testing. My background in econometric training, machine learning experience, and financial domain knowledge positions me well to contribute across these tracks, including risk assessment of new products and digital risk analytics.
I am diligent, responsible, and accustomed to working efficiently under pressure, with a proven ability to learn quickly and adapt to new environments. I am highly motivated to contribute to CICC's risk management work in Hong Kong, and I am equally open to joining as a summer intern first, should that be the preferred entry path.

Thank you for considering my application!
I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my background could contribute to CICC's Risk Management Department.
