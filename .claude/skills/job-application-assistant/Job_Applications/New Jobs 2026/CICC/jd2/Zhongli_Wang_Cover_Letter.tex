\textbf{Application for International Business Analyst}

\vspace{0.1cm}

\noindent Dear CICC Recruiting Team,

I have successfully completed my Ph.D. in Economics at Bielefeld University (defense: September 21, 2026), following an M.A. in Banking \& Finance from the University of Zurich and a double bachelor's degree in Finance and German Language and Literature from Nankai University. As a native Chinese speaker with C1-level English and German, and with over eight years of study and work experience across China, Switzerland, the Netherlands, Germany, and Italy, I am deeply familiar with both Chinese and international business environments. The International Business Analyst position at CICC's Business Development Department is an ideal match for my background in international market research, financial analysis, and cross-cultural communication.

My experience aligns directly with the responsibilities of this role:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{International market research:} My doctoral research analyzes ICT industry dynamics in Europe and North America, tracking overseas-listed companies (AT\&T, T-Mobile), evaluating market structure, competitive behavior, and cross-border business opportunities. I monitor regulatory and policy developments affecting European markets and incorporate geopolitical and economic dynamics into my analysis.
    \item \textbf{Financial and industry analysis:} I have published peer-reviewed research applying machine learning methods to financial statement analysis and fraud detection, demonstrating strong ability to analyze company fundamentals and market indicators. My internships at the People's Bank of China and the Agricultural Bank of China provided first-hand understanding of China's financial system, regulatory environment, and banking operations.
    \item \textbf{International client development and communication:} At LucidMinds (Amsterdam), I conducted market research on consumer preferences and delivered scenario analyses and trade-off summaries to international business stakeholders in English. I am experienced in building relationships and presenting findings clearly to clients and senior stakeholders across cultures.
    \item \textbf{Analytical and PC skills:} I am proficient in Excel, Word, and PowerPoint, with advanced skills in Python, R, and SQL for data analysis and visualization. I combine strong quantitative abilities with clear, concise business writing in both English and Chinese.
\end{itemize}

I am particularly drawn to CICC's internationalization strategy: supporting regional market research, tracking overseas-listed companies, and developing the international client service platform. My bicultural background positions me well to evaluate collaboration opportunities between local and Chinese enterprises and to communicate effectively with international clients.
I am proactive, responsible, and accustomed to working efficiently under pressure, with a proven ability to learn quickly and adapt to new environments. I am highly motivated to contribute to CICC's international business development in Hong Kong or Singapore, and I am equally open to joining as a summer intern first, should that be the preferred entry path.

Thank you for considering my application!
I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my background could contribute to CICC's Business Development Department.
