\textbf{Application for Staff Economist, Marketplace Economics}

\vspace{0.1cm}

\noindent Dear GetYourGuide recruiting team,

I have successfully completed my Ph.D. in Economics at Bielefeld University, with my defense on September 21, 2026, 
and I hold an M.A. in Business and Economics with a major in Banking and Finance and a minor in Data Science. 
My doctoral research focuses on marketplace economics and industrial organization, 
specifically analyzing strategic firm behavior, pricing dynamics, and market design in two-sided platforms, 
providing a strong foundation for marketplace economics and causal inference work.

I am particularly drawn to GetYourGuide's Marketplace Economics team because of its focus on applying rigorous structural modeling and causal inference to answer fundamental marketplace questions about pricing, demand, supply dynamics, and market design. 
The opportunity to own the economics of pricing end-to-end, from demand estimation and willingness-to-pay analysis to structural modeling and counterfactual simulation, aligns perfectly with my research approach: 
develop theoretical frameworks grounded in economic theory, collect and analyze large-scale data, apply appropriate econometric and causal inference methods, 
validate assumptions rigorously, and communicate complex economic insights clearly to business stakeholders.
My relevant experience comprises:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Structural modeling and market design:} My doctoral research develops structural models to analyze firm pricing and investment decisions in two-sided markets, with focus on how commission structures, incentive mechanisms, and market design features affect equilibrium outcomes. I conduct counterfactual simulations to evaluate policy alternatives and market design changes.
    \item \textbf{Causal inference and empirical methods:} I have extensive experience with econometric methods, parameter estimation, and statistical analysis using Stata, R, and Python. I apply techniques including computational simulations and machine learning methods to large-scale datasets, demonstrating proficiency in rigorous empirical analysis and evidence-based insights.
    \item \textbf{Demand estimation and pricing:} I develop discrete choice and demand estimation models to analyze consumer behavior and willingness to pay, estimate structural parameters, and test theoretical predictions against empirical data. 
    % \item \textbf{Communication and drafting:} I have authored peer-reviewed publications, prepared technical reports, and presented complex economic arguments at international conferences. I have strong drafting skills in both English and German, with experience translating technical analysis into accessible insights for diverse audiences.
\end{itemize}

I have excellent command of both German and English at C1 level, and I am highly motivated to apply my expertise to marketplace problems. 
I am eager to contribute to GetYourGuide's marketplace economics challenges, develop deep expertise in travel and experience goods markets, and work collaboratively with business leaders across Marketing, Pricing, and Supply. 

Thank you so much for considering my application! 
I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my background and abilities could contribute to GetYourGuide's Marketplace Economics team and help unlock value through rigorous economic analysis.
