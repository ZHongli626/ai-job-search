\textbf{Application for Senior Associate - Competition Economics}

\vspace{0.1cm}

\noindent Dear E.CA recruiting team,

I am completing a Ph.D. in Economics at Bielefeld University, with my defense scheduled for September 21, 2026, 
and I hold an M.A. in Business and Economics with a major in Banking and Finance and a minor in Data Science. 
My doctoral research focuses on computational economics and industrial organization, 
specifically analyzing strategic firm behavior, market structure, and competitive dynamics in ICT markets, 
providing a strong foundation for competition economics consulting.

I am particularly drawn to E.CA Economics because of its reputation for combining rigorous economic theory with data-driven empirical analysis to address complex competition issues. 
The opportunity to apply economic theory, econometrics, and quantitative methods to support clients in merger reviews, 
abuse of dominance cases, and regulatory proceedings aligns perfectly with my research approach: 
develop theoretical frameworks, test hypotheses with appropriate data and econometric methods, 
validate assumptions rigorously, and communicate complex economic arguments clearly to diverse audiences including authorities and clients.
My relevant experience comprises:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Industrial organization and market analysis:} My doctoral research develops computational models to analyze strategic firm behavior, capacity investment decisions, market structure, and competitive dynamics in ICT markets. I study how network effects, service capacity, and strategic interactions shape market outcomes, which are directly relevant to competition economics.
    \item \textbf{Econometric and quantitative analysis:} I have extensive experience with econometric methods, parameter estimation, and statistical analysis using Stata, R, and Python. In published research, I applied machine learning methods (XGBoost, Random Forest) to large-scale datasets, demonstrating proficiency in data-driven empirical analysis.
    \item \textbf{Economic modeling and theory:} I develop agent-based models to analyze firm strategies under uncertainty, estimate structural parameters, and test theoretical predictions against empirical data. This experience translates directly to building economic models for competition cases and merger simulations.
    % \item \textbf{Communication and drafting:} I have authored peer-reviewed publications, prepared technical reports, and presented complex economic arguments at international conferences. I have strong drafting skills in both English and German, with experience translating technical analysis into accessible insights for diverse audiences.
\end{itemize}

I have excellent command of both German and English at C1 level, and I am a native Mandarin Chinese speaker. 
I am highly motivated to apply my expertise in industrial organization, econometrics, and quantitative modeling to competition economics consulting. 
I am eager to contribute to E.CA's high-profile competition cases, develop deep subject-matter expertise across different sectors, and work in your supportive, flat-hierarchy team environment. 
I feel ready to engage with clients, authorities, and legal teams, and to continue developing my technical and communication skills in this dynamic field.

Thank you so much for considering my application! 
I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my background and abilities could contribute to E.CA Economics' leading competition practice.
