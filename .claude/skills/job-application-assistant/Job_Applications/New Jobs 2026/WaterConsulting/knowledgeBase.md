### 4. Asset managers vs. PE funds vs. hedge funds vs. AI/high-growth tech (quick primer)

- **Asset managers** (e.g., traditional long-only, offshore fixed income): manage pooled client money against benchmarks and mandates; talent needs emphasize portfolio management, credit research, macro allocation, and client-facing investment specialists. Regulated under SFC in HK. Compensation is steadier, bonus tied to AUM and performance.
- **Private-equity funds**: buy whole companies, hold 3-7 years, exit; hiring revolves around deal execution, due diligence and operating partners. Fewer seats, high selectivity, carried-interest upside.
- **Hedge funds**: absolute-return, flexible strategies, faster cycles; talent needs skew toward quant researchers, PMs with track records, and execution specialists. Pay is highly performance-linked.
- **AI/high-growth tech**: venture-backed companies scaling fast; hire data scientists and ML engineers plus leaders who can build teams from zero; equity-heavy compensation; mandate complexity includes both technical depth and founder temperament.

Useful line: "I can genuinely speak the language of both your financial-services and technology clients — my M.A. is in banking and finance, my research is ML-heavy, and I'm a TTPS-approved native Mandarin speaker, which maps directly onto cross-border mandates."
