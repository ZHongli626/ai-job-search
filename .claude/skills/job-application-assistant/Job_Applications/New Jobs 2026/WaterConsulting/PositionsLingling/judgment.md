Prepare a concise evidence-based pitch: “I work on computational economics and simulation using Python; my research includes supervised machine learning applied to financial fraud and automated research workflows. 3. Prepare one technical research case explaining problem formulation, data, modelling, validation, limitations and reproducibility. 4. For each prospective role confirm exact location, employment entity, open status, seniority, market data and coding requirements, visa sponsorship, compensation structure and whether research or client coverage dominates the work.
