## Short connection request

Hello [Name], I am exploring suitable opportunities in Hong Kong and would value your advice on the best fit for my background. I expect to visit Hong Kong in December 2026 and would be glad to connect and meet if convenient.

## Customization before sending

- Replace `[target function/industry]` with a specific area, such as investment management, financial services, executive search, HR, technology or business development.
- Add one sentence describing your strongest relevant experience or achievement.
- Attach your CV only after checking that it is tailored to the target role.
- Use "December 2026" only if that timing is still correct.
- Avoid saying "I can do the role quite well"; demonstrate this through one specific result or capability instead.
