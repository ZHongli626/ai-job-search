Ad text:
The Kiel Institute for the World Economy seeks applications for a full-time PostDoc position in quantitative economics. The position is part of a new Leibniz-funded transfer project on AI and structural economic models for economic policy.

Your Role

Your main role is to produce high-quality research. You will work with structural economic models in fields such as international trade, macroeconomics, finance, public, spatial or climate economics, apply them to current policy questions, and help assess how reliable AI-assisted model analysis is. You will work closely with policy partners. Beyond the project, you will have freedom and support to pursue your own research agenda toward publication in leading journals.

Your Profile

A PhD in economics or a closely related field, completed by the start of the contract.
Research in a quantitative field of economics, for example macroeconomics, finance, international trade, public, spatial or climate economics, with structural or quantitative models.
Strong programming skills in a scientific language (e.g., Julia, Python, R, MATLAB, GAMS).
Good software practice: version control (Git), testing and reproducible code.
A research agenda with potential for publications in leading international journals.
Very good English. German is useful but not required.
Experience with DSGE/HANK models (e.g., MacroModelling.jl, Dynare) or with quantitative trade and CGE models is an asset.
Experience with LLM-based tools is an asset.
Experience designing validation or evaluation studies is an asset.
Experience with policy institutions is an asset.
We are offering

There is no teaching obligation and only a very light administrative burden. The contract runs until the end of the project (27 months) and starts in autumn 2027 (negotiable). The salary follows the German public sector pay scale (TV-L E13, 100%), approximately €56k–€79k gross per year depending on experience, with full social security coverage, including health insurance. The Institute offers a family-friendly environment, including paid parental leave in line with German law (with contracts extended for the duration of parental leave).
