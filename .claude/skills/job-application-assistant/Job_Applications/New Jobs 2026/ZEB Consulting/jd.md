Consultant - quantitative Methods & Strategy (f|m|x) 

Berlin, Berlin, Germany

zeb is one of the leading strategy, management and IT consultancies specializing in financial services in Europe. We support banks, insurance companies and (tech) service providers in dealing with all the challenges and opportunities arising from transformation in the industry.

Your contribution to a modern financial industry:

Strategic consulting: You will support our clients with bank-wide risk management and risk controlling issues – from risk identification and measurement through to effective management. In doing so, you will become an important contact person for risk-related issues and actively help shape our clients’ risk management. 
Data-driven analyses and modeling: You will design and conduct analyses and modeling in regard to various risk topics, e.g. ESG risks, RWA drivers or scenario analyses, and translate complex risk and management issues into transparent decision-making bases for management teams. 
Responsibility and project work: You will work actively in interdisciplinary project teams and take responsibility for your own work packages. Collaborating closely with experienced colleagues and our clients, you will drive projects forward and design future-proof solutions. 
Preparation and presentation of results: You will make complex content understandable, prepare your results for the respective target audience and present them to our international clients from the financial industry. 
Knowledge development and transfer: You will continuously expand your specialist knowledge in the banking and risk management environment, develop innovative solutions as part of a team and contribute to studies, expert articles and internal publications. 

Your skills:

Excellent university degree in mathematics, natural sciences, e.g. physics, or economics, e.g. political economics, business administration or business informatics. 
Understanding of and interest in the financial industry as well as in current challenges in banking (e.g. AI and digitalization) and regulatory expertise. 
Enthusiasm for analysis and for translating your results into clear and actionable recommendations for management teams. 
Confident use of consulting tools, especially Excel and PowerPoint; experience in using programming and data analysis tools (e.g. Python, R, SQL, VBA) as well as visualization and reporting tools (e.g. Tableau, Power BI) is a plus. 
Excellent command of the German language (at least C1 level, business-fluent proficiency) and good English language skills (at least B2 level, fluent), both written and spoken.
