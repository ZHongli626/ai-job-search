\noindent Dear zeb recruiting team,

I have great interest in the Consultant position in Quantitative Methods \& Strategy in Berlin.

\begin{itemize}

    \item \textbf{Data and analytical tools:} I work with Python, R, Julia, Mathematica, SQL, econometrics, machine learning, simulation, SMM, and MCMC, and I produce reproducible analyses with clear assumptions and documented results.
    \item \textbf{Communication and collaboration:} I have presented research at international conferences and workshops, developed teaching materials, organized academic events, and worked across China and Europe.
\end{itemize}

I command both German and English at C1 level, and I am a native Mandarin Chinese speaker. 
I am motivated to apply this international perspective in interdisciplinary project teams and to develop further expertise in banking regulation, 
risk controlling, ESG risks, RWA drivers, and scenario analysis.
