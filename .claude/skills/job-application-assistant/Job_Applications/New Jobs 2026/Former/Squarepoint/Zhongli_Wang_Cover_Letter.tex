\noindent Dear Hiring Team,

I am writing to apply for the Junior Quant Researcher position at Squarepoint. 
I am completing the final stage of my Ph.D. in Economics at Bielefeld University, with my defense scheduled for September 21, 2026, 
and I also hold an M.A. in Business and Economics with a Banking and Finance major and a B.Sc. in Finance. 
My background includes statistical modeling, computational research, financial data analysis, 
and cross-region policy analysis.

I am very interested in this role because it deals with research, implementation, 
and daily discipline inside an automated trading framework. 
I would like to contribute my abilities to a team that turns large, noisy datasets into trading ideas that can be tested, monitored, and improved. 
My preferred primary work location is Hong Kong, while the final arrangement is open to discussion according to Squarepoint's business needs.

My closest fit with the mandate is the ability to connect quantitative modeling with careful empirical execution. 
In my doctoral work, I build computational models of strategic firm behavior, estimate parameters, and analyze how market structure changes outcomes under uncertainty. 
In a published fraud detection project, I used accounting, financial, and market indicators with XGBoost and LightGBM to identify red flags in listed companies.

I would bring the following strengths to Squarepoint's quantitative research team:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Research and implementation:} Econometrics, machine learning, SMM, MCMC, and agent-based modeling for complex market environments.
    \item \textbf{Large-scale data analysis:} Advanced Python skills, plus R, Julia, Mathematica, SQL foundations, and reproducible financial and behavioral data analysis.
    \item \textbf{Market-structure perspective:} Finance training and research on competition, network effects, capacity constraints, and data-driven markets.
    \item \textbf{Operational discipline:} Experience checking assumptions, validating output, documenting processes, presenting results under scrutiny, and working under time pressure.
\end{itemize}

My academic and professional training is aligned with the analytical core of the role: 
statistical inference, programming, financial markets, and disciplined model validation. 
I would aim to contribute through documented research pipelines, dataset analysis for tradable patterns, 
pre-market data checks, and careful monitoring of strategy behavior.

Thank you so much for your time and consideration! 
I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my research background, financial training, 
and coding experience can contribute to Squarepoint's Junior Quant Researcher mandate.
