The School  Superiore Sant'Anna of Pisa, with D.R. no. 667 of September 18, 2026 , announces a public selection for the awarding of one two-year research contract pursuant to Article 22 of Law 240/2010 at the Academic  Faculty of Social Sciences - Institute of Economics, in the Groups of Academic disciplines  13/ECON-01 - Economics, Academic discipline ECON-01/A - Economics.

Applications for participation in the selection must be submitted exclusively after registration on the dedicated IT application, available from September,  23, at the link 

https://pica.cineca.it/santanna/2026-cdr-14/ 

Deadline: November 6, 2026, at 12:00 Italian Time.

For reporting exclusively technical problems, contact CINECA support via the link at the bottom of the page  https://pica.cineca.it/santanna

The interview with the committee  will be in Pisa on December 3, 2026. 

The list of candidates admitted to the interview will be published on November 24, 2026.

The applicants resident or working abroad, admitted  to the interviews, are allowed to hold the interview in their country of residence via conference call if previously identified in an Italian Embassy/consulate or in a recognized University/Research Centre by an employee or a Professor. Please contact contrattiricerca@santannapisa.it) not after 72 hours before the date of the interview. 

The public discussion can be attended by all interested parties.
