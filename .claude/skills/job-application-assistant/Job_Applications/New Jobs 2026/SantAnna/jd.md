1️⃣ 𝗦𝗖𝗜-𝗣𝗘𝗧 – Principal investigator: Elisa Palagi

“Socioeconomic and Climate Inequality: Understanding Disparities in Emissions, Climate Impacts and Perceptions to Design Policy for an Equitable Transition”, funded by the Fondo Italiano per la Scienza (FIS).

The position will focus on the distributional impacts of climate change and climate policy, using a combination of climate econometric and survey-based methods.

2️⃣ 𝗧𝗥𝗔𝗖𝗘 – Principal investigator: Matteo Coronese

“Tracking Regional Asymmetries and Climate inEqualities”, funded by the Fondo Italiano per la Scienza (FIS).

The position will focus on quantitative methods for distributional climate-impact analysis, including the integration of climate, geospatial and socioeconomic data and the reconstruction and analysis of subnational income and wealth distributions.
