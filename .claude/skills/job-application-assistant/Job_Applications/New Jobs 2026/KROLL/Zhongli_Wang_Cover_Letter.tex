\textbf{Application for Analyst, Competition and Regulatory Economics}

\vspace{0.1cm}

\noindent Dear Kroll recruiting team,

I have successfully completed my Ph.D. in Economics at Bielefeld University, with my defense on September 21, 2026, 
and I hold an M.A. in Business and Economics with a major in Banking and Finance and a minor in Data Science. 
My doctoral research focuses on computational economics and industrial organization, 
specifically analyzing strategic firm behavior, market structure, and competitive dynamics in ICT markets, 
providing a strong foundation for competition and regulatory economics analysis.

I am particularly drawn to Kroll's Competition and Regulatory Economics practice because of its reputation for applying rigorous economic and econometric analysis to complex competition, regulatory and commercial disputes. 
The opportunity to support clients in antitrust investigations, merger reviews, damages quantification, litigation, regulatory proceedings and international arbitration aligns perfectly with my research approach: 
develop theoretical frameworks, collect and analyze qualitative and quantitative data, test hypotheses with appropriate econometric methods, 
validate assumptions rigorously, and communicate complex economic arguments clearly to clients, authorities and courts.
My relevant experience comprises:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Economic and market analysis:} My doctoral research develops computational models to analyze strategic firm behavior, capacity investment decisions, market structure, and competitive dynamics in ICT markets. I study how network effects, service capacity, and strategic interactions shape market outcomes, which are directly relevant to competition and regulatory analysis.
    \item \textbf{Econometric and quantitative analysis:} I have extensive experience with econometric methods, parameter estimation, and statistical analysis using Stata, R, and Python. In published research, I applied machine learning methods to large-scale datasets, demonstrating proficiency in data-driven empirical analysis and evidence-based case support.
    \item \textbf{Economic modeling and theory:} I develop agent-based models to analyze firm strategies under uncertainty, estimate structural parameters, and test theoretical predictions against empirical data. 
    % \item \textbf{Communication and drafting:} I have authored peer-reviewed publications, prepared technical reports, and presented complex economic arguments at international conferences. I have strong drafting skills in both English and German, with experience translating technical analysis into accessible insights for diverse audiences.
\end{itemize}

I have excellent command of both German and English at C1 level. 
I am highly motivated to apply my expertise in industrial organization, econometrics, and quantitative modeling to competition and regulatory economics. 
I am eager to contribute to Kroll's high-profile competition and regulatory cases, develop deep subject-matter expertise across different sectors, and work collaboratively with experienced economists and industry experts. 
I feel ready to engage with clients, authorities, and legal teams, and to continue developing my technical and communication skills in this dynamic field.

Thank you so much for considering my application! 
I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my background and abilities could contribute to Kroll's leading competition and regulatory economics practice.
