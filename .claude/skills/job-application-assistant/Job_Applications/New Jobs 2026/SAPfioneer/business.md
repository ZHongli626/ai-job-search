1
The only fintech powered by a worldclass ERP platform
Fioneer was spun off from SAP to do one thing exceptionally well: serve banks and insurers. Built on SAP’s strong technology foundation but operating independently, Fioneer is fully focused on helping financial institutions succeed in a complex, fast-moving, and highly regulated environment.

The result is focused innovation that addresses critical customer pain points, seamless integration across diverse IT landscapes beyond the SAP universe, and an experience-led vision for the future of financial services.


2
Unique platform: rich functionality end-to-end and front-to-back at the same time
Fioneer platforms deliver rich, end-to-end functionality across key business processes of banks and insurers. Instead of stitching together various disconnected systems, financial institutions gain unified platforms with comprehensive out-of-the-box capabilities for next-generation financial services.

A unique differentiator is Fioneer’s fully integrated front to back integration. By connecting channels, operations, finance, and reporting seamlessly, Fioneer helps institutions improve reliability, strengthen control, and gain the transparency needed to transform with confidence.

3
The only core system with fully integrated finance
“Book it and it works” means full automation from process and booking through position management, posting, and reconciliation.

We go beyond the subledger, delivering the classification and control framework that enables true front-to-back consistency, strong control, and compliance built for continuous proof, not periodic submission.


4
Simplicity and speed: Fioneer native, clean and open core
Our modular, composable, cloud-agnostic architecture is built on clean-core principles and native event streaming, enabling seamless composability, no-friction upgrades, and real-time agility.

This gives Fioneer a distinct position in the market: more mature, scalable, and enterprise-ready than digital-native challenger platforms, yet more flexible, leaner, and future-ready than the traditional competition.


Open core provides full access to applications and ecosystem connectivity through built-in domain APIs, a unified integration and extensibility layer, and reusable data products.

Fioneer stands apart with an open architecture where APIs are embedded by design, not just added as connectors. This makes integration, extension, and reuse simpler, particularly across complex IT environments.

5
Your choice of AI agents. Secured from within
Adopt and evolve the AI agents that fit your business while keeping sensitive data and critical systems protected. A dedicated, financial-services-specific agentic layer connects AI capabilities to your processes through rule-based guardrails, existing authorization logic, and controlled API access.

This gives you the freedom to innovate with AI – on a secure, governed foundation built for regulated operations.

AI-first delivery best enabled by the software creator, with 25 years of experience
Fioneer’s AI-first delivery model changes the economics of software delivery by accelerating, automating, and scaling execution across the lifecycle. Purpose-built agents for the Fioneer ecosystem remove friction from the implementation work that most often slows projects down and drives up cost, complexity, and risk.

Only Fioneer can evolve the software and the agents to work in lockstep to make AI-first delivery faster, more reliable, reusable, and scalable.

The result is software shaped by customer reality—not theory.
