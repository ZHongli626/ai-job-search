About the Role
As a CBRE Business Data Analyst, you will provide analytical support through data collection, management, and analysis to drive business insights and decision-making. This role is part of the Data Science & Analytics function and focuses on analyzing data to improve effectiveness, predict outcomes, and develop actionable business intelligence.

What You'll Do
Support data collection, input, aggregation, and auditing activities.
Ensure accurate key metric reporting and data review.
Review information accuracy and respond to requests from management and vendors.
Assist in generating reports, dashboards, and ad-hoc presentations.
Respond to information requests in a timely manner.
Gather and analyze data to identify and solve complex business problems.
Recommend new techniques and process improvements where appropriate.
Support teams by providing analytical insights and data-driven recommendations.
Develop and maintain data models, reporting systems, automation tools, dashboards, and performance metrics.
Collaborate with cross-functional stakeholders to understand and support data requirements.
What You'll Need
Bachelor Degree with 3 years of relevant experience.
Strong understanding of processes, procedures, systems, and concepts within the function.
Ability to evaluate and communicate complex information logically and concisely.
Proficiency in Microsoft Office applications, including Word, Excel, and Outlook.
Strong organizational skills and an inquisitive mindset.
Advanced mathematical and analytical abilities.
Experience with data visualization tools and techniques.
Strong problem-solving skills.
Ability to work independently and manage multiple priorities.
Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
