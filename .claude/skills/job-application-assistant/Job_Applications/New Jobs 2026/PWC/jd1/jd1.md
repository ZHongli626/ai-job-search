About Digital Assurance (Including digital assurance, capital market related internal control/data due diligence services, non-audit assurance engagements,crypto services, etc) 



Digital Assurance aims to support the financial audit teams and clients in providing efficient and high-quality assurance and advisory services for example, information systems and data auditing, internal control review, data analytics, and security services, helping clients make informed decisions and build trust in the changing environment.



If you’re a curious thinker who enjoys problem solving and making an impact in an evolving profession, Digital Assurance is the right fit for you.





Job description and responsibilities 



Today's business environment is more complex and more connected. Our clients not only face new and unknown risks, but also new and untapped opportunities. PwC Digital Assurance is at the forefront of this change, ready to provide our clients with various digital services and build trust.



Working with our leading clients in key industries in the Digital Assurance team, you will gain exposure to:



Digital assurance 

Information system audit and data audit, assisting our financial audit team in providing assurance services related to information systems and data


Internal control/data due diligence and consulting services related to the capital market

Provide due diligence and consulting services for clients' internal controls/data in response to different capital market requirements


Non-Audit Assurance services

Provide assurance services other than financial statement auditing based on customer needs such as cloud services, outsourced business processes and centralised operations.


Digital Asset services (Digital Assurance in Hong Kong SAR, China) 

Provide a full range of services to both our digital asset clients as well as traditional/financial services clients that are incorporating digital assets in their businesses.
