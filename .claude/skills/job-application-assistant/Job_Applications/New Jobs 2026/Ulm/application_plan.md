# Ulm Early Career Excellence Fellowship: application plan

## Bottom line

This is a **faculty nomination** for the University of Ulm's Early Career Excellence Fellowship, rather than an application that the candidate can submit independently. The faculty, the proposed host unit, and the candidate must coordinate the nomination.

Based on the current candidate profile, there is a likely eligibility blocker: the nomination form requires **at least one year of relevant research experience after the doctorate by 13 November 2026**, while the profile lists the PhD defense as **21 September 2026** and does not list postdoctoral employment. Confirm this with the nominating faculty before spending time on the full proposal.

## Decision gate: confirm eligibility first

- [ ] Ask the prospective Ulm host and faculty whether the candidate is eligible despite the post-PhD experience requirement.
- [ ] Confirm the official date of the doctoral degree award, not only the defense date.
- [ ] Confirm whether any documented interruption or extension period can be counted under the fellowship rules. The form says the faculty must check and document such periods.
- [X] Confirm that the candidate has **not** completed the doctorate at Ulm University.
- [X] Confirm that the candidate has **not** been employed as a postdoc at Ulm University after completing the doctorate.
- [ ] Confirm that the doctoral degree will have been completed no more than three years before 13 November 2026.
- [ ] Confirm that the candidate can take up an independent research project at a University of Ulm host institution and satisfy the later employment requirements.

If the faculty confirms that the one-year requirement cannot be waived or credited, do not submit this nomination in 2026. Ask whether a later call or another Ulm position is more suitable.

## Who must be involved

The nomination form must be completed in coordination with:

- the nominating University of Ulm faculty, which makes the scientific assessment and selection;
- the receiving institute and research group;
- a named institutional supervisor (Institutsleitung or Dean);
- a named mentor, who commits to supporting the fellow's scientific and professional development; and
- the candidate, who must approve the nomination, research project, qualification concept, and data transmission.

The current email (`jd.md`) should be treated as an introduction/referral, not as the complete job advertisement. Obtain the official call, host-unit details, and faculty-specific internal deadline from the contact who sent it.

## Required actions

### 1. Secure a host and faculty nomination

- [ ] Identify a University of Ulm professor/research group whose research fits the proposed project.
- [ ] Send the host a short research summary, CV, publication list, and the eligibility questions above.
- [ ] Agree on the host institute, professorship/research group, supervisor, mentor, and faculty.
- [ ] Ask for the faculty's internal selection process and deadline. The official external submission deadline is **Friday, 13 November 2026**.
- [ ] Clarify whether the faculty wants the candidate to draft sections of the form or only supply supporting material.

### 2. Develop the independent research project

Prepare a maximum **two-page** project description covering:

- central research question and objectives;
- scientific approach and planned methods;
- innovation potential;
- expected scientific results; and
- feasibility within the **24-month** fellowship period.

The project should be clearly independent while fitting the Ulm host's research area. A plausible starting direction, grounded in the current profile, is an independent computational/agent-based research project connecting economic modeling, heterogeneous agents, data value chains, or AI-assisted economic research. The final topic must be agreed with the host and should not be presented as an Ulm-specific fact until verified.

### 3. Plan the follow-on grant

Prepare a maximum **half-page** section stating:

- the competitive funding programme to target; and
- the expected submission date.

Explain how the 24-month fellowship will produce the preliminary results, methods, collaborations, and project maturity needed for an independent junior research group or comparable grant. The future grant must be submitted with the University of Ulm as host institution.

### 4. Obtain the host qualification concept

The receiving institution must provide a maximum **one-page** concept explaining:

- scientific integration and fit with the host research area;
- support for an independent scientific profile and shared objectives;
- available rooms, infrastructure, and any required laboratory capacity;
- support for preparing and submitting the follow-on grant; and
- mentoring arrangements.

This section cannot be completed by the candidate alone; coordinate it with the host and mentor.

### 5. Prepare the budget

Create a preliminary budget with a total of no more than **€30,000**. It may include:

- consumables/materials;
- project-related investments;
- research assistants;
- travel;
- individual scientific career development, up to **€5,000**; and
- other project-related costs.

Allocate amounts across 2027, 2028, and 2029 and add a short justification for each cost category. The form notes that later spending remains subject to University of Ulm budget and procurement rules.

### 6. Supply nomination material

Provide the faculty with:

- an up-to-date academic CV including a publication list;
- doctoral degree details and the official completion date;
- a concise record of relevant research experience after the doctorate, if any;
- the research-project draft;
- the follow-on grant plan;
- any information needed for the host qualification concept and budget; and
- confirmation of willingness to be nominated and to sign the candidate declaration.

The form states that no additional attachments are required beyond the scientific CV with publication list, but confirm whether the faculty requests internal supporting documents.

## Form sections the faculty must complete

1. Candidate details and postdoctoral research experience.
2. Receiving unit, supervisor, mentor, and contact details.
3. Research project, maximum two pages.
4. Follow-on grant, maximum half a page.
5. Host qualification concept, maximum one page.
6. Budget plan, maximum €30,000.
7. Faculty's nomination justification, maximum one page.
8. Faculty recruitment and selection process, maximum half a page.
9. Faculty confirmations of eligibility, infrastructure, supervision, and employment-process notice.
10. Academic CV and publication list.
11. Binding signature by the faculty.
12. Candidate's signature and consent.

## Submission and timeline

- **Immediately:** resolve the eligibility issue and identify the host/faculty contact.
- **Before the faculty's internal deadline:** agree on the project, budget, host concept, mentor, and nomination justification.
- **Before 13 November 2026:** faculty completes and submits the signed nomination form and academic CV/publication list electronically to `protrainu@uni-ulm.de`.
- **Before submission:** candidate reviews the form, confirms the project and qualification concept, and signs the candidate confirmation.

## Questions to send to the contact

1. Can a candidate whose PhD defense is scheduled for 21 September 2026 satisfy the requirement of one year of relevant research experience after the doctorate by 13 November 2026?
2. What is the exact faculty-internal nomination deadline and selection procedure?
3. Which Ulm institute, professorship, supervisor, and mentor would host the project?
4. Is there an official call or guidance document in addition to the attached nomination form?
5. What follow-on funding programmes does the faculty expect candidates to target?
