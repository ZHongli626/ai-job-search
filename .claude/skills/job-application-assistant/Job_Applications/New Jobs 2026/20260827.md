Company Development History
Founded in 2010: IWin Wealth Management, our parent company, stands as one of China’s earliest independent third-party wealth management institutions. It pioneered the introduction and implementation of internationally standardized financial planning advisory models, serving clients with an impartial and objective stance.



In 2013: the company obtained a Hong Kong insurance brokerage license and launched Version 1.0 of its intelligent financial planning system following dedicated research and development. It also secured a private fund manager license in mainland China, expanding into family funds and fund of funds (FOF) sectors. As an industry trailblazer, it proactively and comprehensively exited non-standard fixed-income products.

Between 2015 and 2017: the company acquired a mainland insurance agency license and established Anying Overseas, an immigration and study abroad consultancy. During this period, it launched an intelligent retirement planning system. As a founding vice-chair member, it co-initiated the Shenzhen Family Office Promotion Association. Its distinctive advisory service model earned widespread industry acclaim and numerous prestigious awards.

From 2018 to 2019: the company innovatively proposed the FIT model for family offices, driving robust growth in family trust services. Concurrently, it released Version 3.0 of the comprehensive financial planning system and Version 1.0 of the family wealth management system. It also launched the Certified Chinese Financial Planner (CCFP) program and the Certified Chinese Retirement Planner (CCRP) program.

During 2020–2022: the company was honored with the Annual Outstanding Innovation Team award at the Forbes China Insurance Summit. It introduced the "Three Accounts" concept for global asset allocation, while its family fund offerings, EAM services, and businesses in Bermuda, Singapore and other jurisdictions sustained steady growth. An advanced family office program was also introduced during this phase.

In 2023–2024: the Hong Kong IFA team was formally established, alongside the completion and launch of a brand-new team office. This milestone marked the commencement of a new chapter in the company’s development.
