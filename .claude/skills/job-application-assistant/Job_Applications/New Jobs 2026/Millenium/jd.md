Job Description: Quantitative Researcher

Job Function Summary

We are a systematic trading team dedicated to building a machine-driven investment pipeline that can tackle problems of scale far beyond the reach of traditional systems. Even though we are operating in the financial industry, we are committed to pushing the boundary of human knowledge in related fields such as mathematics, statistics, and machine learning in order to achieve our mission.

We are looking for a talented quantitative researcher to join our team. Successful candidate should be capable of developing novel techniques, building unique insights, and making robust systems that will be used in our strategy.  One must be able to combine strong technical skills, a passion for creative problem solving, and an intense curiosity about financial markets, science, and human behavior.

Principal Responsibilities

Use disciplined scientific approach to develop quantitative investment models
Research and develop novel machine learning algorithms
Develop automated investment models based on peer-reviewed research papers
Develop unique insights into various datasets
Use mathematical tools to analyze and optimize trades
Quantitatively measure and control risk of portfolio
Apply scientific methods to improve efficiency of trading pipeline
Qualifications/Skills Required

Undergraduates, Masters or doctorate degree in quantitative disciplines, like statistics, mathematics, physics, electrical engineering, or computer science
Capable of utilizing sophisticated mathematical tools and generalizing it to different contexts
Strong programming skill in at least one programming language (like Python, C++, or Java)
Experience analyzing real world problems with disciplined scientific approach
Capable of following state-of-the-art machine learning and statistics research with no guidance
Curiosity driven mindset with ability to explore uncharted territory
Highly motivated and willing to take ownership of work
