# bobco GmbH: company, financial and salary assessment

**Research date:** 17 November 2026  
**Role:** Full-Stack ERP Consultant für Prozessberatung & Odoo Implementierung

## Overall judgment

**Worth continuing the interview process, with medium confidence in the business opportunity and low confidence in its financial resilience.** bobco has a recognisable niche, an established Odoo partner listing and named customer references. It is also a young, small consultancy whose revenue, profitability and cash position could not be verified from the sources available. It could offer rapid learning and responsibility, but the breadth of the role and the company's limited financial transparency make careful questions important before accepting an offer.

For Zhongli, the role is a plausible route into business transformation and technology consulting. It is less directly aligned with a career centred on quantitative economics, research or machine learning: check how much of the actual work involves analytical problem-solving versus configuration, customer coordination and project delivery.

## Company and prospects

- **Identity:** bobco GmbH is a Berlin company registered as HRB 257617 B; its [imprint](https://itsbob.co/de/imprint) names Ferry Kluger and Philip Braun as representatives. Its [LinkedIn page](https://de.linkedin.com/company/itsbob) says it was founded in 2023 and lists 2–10 employees. The supplied job description says a core team of about 10 plus external specialists; these are snapshots, not independently audited headcounts.
- **Business model:** The job description describes process discovery, Odoo implementation and continuing support, particularly for e-commerce and B2B services. Follow-on work could provide recurring demand, but its contractual duration and revenue share are unknown.
- **Evidence of activity:** [Odoo's partner directory](https://www.odoo.com/de_DE/partners/bobco-gmbh-13173790) lists bobco as a Silver partner with 20 references and certified experts. Directory figures are platform-reported snapshots, not audited revenue or proof of active paying customers today. bobco also publishes [named case studies](https://itsbob.co/case-studies); reported customer outcomes are company marketing claims, not independently verified results.
- **Positive outlook:** ERP implementation and ongoing process improvement give the company a coherent service proposition. A small team can provide exposure to clients, decisions and the complete project lifecycle. This is a qualitative judgment, not a forecast of growth.
- **Key risks:** Dependence on Odoo, a small delivery team and potentially a few major clients could amplify platform changes, project delays or customer losses. The actual degree of concentration and pipeline health is unknown. Independent ownership of clients, budgets and delivery could also make the learning curve steep without structured support.

**Negotiation judgment:** For a five-day full-time role, an initial discussion around **EUR 52,000–60,000 gross fixed annually** is a reasonable, non-guaranteed target given your doctoral-level analytical and programming background alongside the absence of direct ERP implementation experience. If they expect immediate independent ownership of projects, budgets and client relationships, seek the upper end or more; if they offer a supported transition with significant training, an offer nearer the lower end may be plausible. The actual budget and offer may differ substantially. Compare the guaranteed base with benchmarks separately from any variable payment.

Suggested wording: “Für eine Vollzeitstelle stelle ich mir – abhängig von Verantwortungsumfang, Einarbeitung und Gesamtpaket – ein fixes Jahresbruttogehalt von etwa 52.000 bis 60.000 Euro vor. Wie ist die Gehaltsspanne für die Rolle vorgesehen?”

For the advertised four-day option, establish actual weekly hours first. If it means 32 rather than 40 hours and pay is prorated, EUR 52,000–60,000 at 40 hours corresponds to **EUR 41,600–48,000** at 32 hours; a compressed 40-hour week is different. Ask about overtime, travel time and reimbursement. Profit sharing is upside unless its formula and payment are contractually guaranteed: request the calculation, eligibility, historical payouts, payment date and treatment on joining or leaving.

## Fit and questions before deciding

Your strengths include Python-based modelling, economic analysis, research on AI workflows, finance training and German/English communication. Relevant gaps include demonstrated Odoo/ERP implementation, commercial project and budget ownership, and hands-on e-commerce or logistics operations. Confirm whether bobco seeks someone who can grow into those responsibilities or expects them on day one.

Ask in the interview:

1. How did revenue and profitability develop over the last two years, and is the company currently profitable? How much work is contractually booked for the next 6–12 months?
2. What share of revenue comes from the largest client and from recurring support? How stable is the project pipeline?
3. What fixed salary range and weekly hours apply? How exactly is profit sharing calculated, and what has actually been paid historically?
4. What does onboarding include, who mentors a first-time Odoo consultant, and when would independent project and budget responsibility begin?
5. How are workload, overtime, customer travel and project overruns managed? What percentage of the job is process discovery, configuration, data/AI work and customer management?
6. How often must someone based in Bielefeld be in Berlin or at client sites, and who pays travel and accommodation? Is this arrangement written into the contract?
7. Is the employment contract permanent, what is the probation period, and how are training and Odoo certification funded?

**Decision rule:** Continue if the team can demonstrate a credible pipeline, supported onboarding, manageable travel and a fixed salary appropriate to the responsibility. Reassess if they expect immediate solo delivery without support or cannot explain compensation and workload clearly. Financial uncertainty alone is not evidence of a problem, but it should inform the level of risk you are willing to accept.
