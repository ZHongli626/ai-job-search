## Bielefeld: suggested order of conversations

1. **Fraunhofer IEM - applied research and AI.** Its [research unit](https://www.iem.fraunhofer.de/en/about-us/research/scientific-automation-research-unit.html) works on simulation, data analytics and machine intelligence. Your computational models, Python and ML offer a credible methodological bridge. Ask whether economists or other quantitative researchers can join applied AI teams. Engineering and control-system knowledge may be required.
2. **IANUS Simulation - simulation and optimisation.** Its [platform](https://ianus-simulation.de/technik-integration) combines simulation, AI and optimisation. Discuss transferable modelling, calibration and surrogate-model work; ask whether positions require fluid mechanics or CFD experience, which your profile does not establish.
3. **Arvato Systems and NTT DATA Business Solutions - data and AI consulting.** Explore data science, AI solutions and quantitative consulting, and ask which teams actually hire Python-oriented researchers. Enterprise platforms, cloud or production deployment may be gaps; confirm role requirements and work location.
4. **Hannover Re - quantitative risk and analytics.** Your finance education, statistical modelling and fraud-detection research give you a useful starting point for a discussion about risk analytics. Ask about actuarial qualifications, insurance experience, location and entry routes for economics PhDs.
5. **KPMG - financial risk and analytics.** Discuss financial statement fraud research and quantitative analysis with relevant advisory teams. Ask about the balance of consulting, audit, programming, travel and any professional qualifications required.
6. **University of Bielefeld and HSBI - research and academic contacts.** Ask about applied research projects and research-industry collaborations. Your existing Bielefeld affiliation is a useful starting point, but does not guarantee an opening.

**Further leads:** CLAAS, Miele, Beckhoff, WAGO, HARTING and Dr. Oetker are worth a second pass for analytics or AI teams. Their presence alone does not establish that those teams are represented or recruiting. Prioritise them if their career pages reveal relevant positions.

## Dortmund: worthwhile alternatives

- **KHS:** ask about data analytics, simulation and AI in industrial processes; check whether roles require automation or engineering experience.
- **WITRON:** explore warehouse software and logistics analytics; its [careers material](https://careers.witron.com/locations/hernando) describes logistics automation. Ask where analytical teams are based and whether they recruit at this stand.
- **DACHSER and DP World Logistics High Tech Europe:** ask about forecasting, operations research and logistics data teams; these are questions to investigate, not confirmed openings.
- **Dortmunder Volksbank:** ask about risk, fraud and data analysis, and whether relevant positions are local or centralised.
- **Stadt Dortmund and IKK classic:** possible public-sector or healthcare analytics conversations; verify the actual functions and work location.

Other listed Dortmund organisations focus on a broad mix of training, transport, retail, care and operational work. Some may have relevant quantitative teams, but the supplied exhibitor descriptions do not demonstrate this. The organiser lists approximately 55 exhibitors overall while its online directory shows 29; the local Dortmund list may therefore be incomplete. Check the [live directory](https://news.jobwoche.de/jobmessen/jobmesse-dortmund-19-11-2026/aussteller/) and [stand plan](https://www.jobwoche.de/img/messen/e/e/ee54a73b43ca03bc868c9c7f81cecc48/rahmenprogramm-und-hallenplan.pdf) for changes.

## Timing and practical choice

- **Bielefeld:** 12:00-16:30, University of Bielefeld, Zentrale Halle, Universitaetsstrasse 25, 33615 Bielefeld. [University event page](https://www.uni-bielefeld.de/themen/career-service/studierende/berufseinstiegsmesse/).
- **Dortmund:** Audi Zentrum Dortmund, Ludwig-Lohner-Strasse 7-9, 44143 Dortmund. The [city event page](https://www.dortmund.de/dortmund-erleben/veranstaltungskalender/termin_147362.html) and [organiser event description](https://www.jobwoche.de/jobmessen/jobmesse-dortmund-19-11-2026/infos/jobmesse-dortmund-2026-job-ausbildung-weiterbildung-2026-01-06) say general visitors may enter 12:30-17:00, although other organiser pages display 09:30-17:00. Confirm access times with the organiser if planning an early arrival. Admission is free; [registration](https://news.jobwoche.de/jobmessen/jobmesse-dortmund-19-11-2026/eintrittskarte/) is recommended.

The general visitor hours largely overlap. Attending both would leave limited conversation time after travel; check live transport before attempting it. Choose Dortmund instead if a relevant employer confirms a meeting or a particularly suitable vacancy there, or if logistics and industrial operations are your primary target.

## Preparation and follow-up

- Before the fair, check the current exhibitor and stand lists and career pages for the first five Bielefeld targets; record relevant role titles and location requirements.
- Prepare a 30-second introduction: economics researcher using Python, agent-based simulation and machine learning, with financial fraud and agentic AI research experience; interested in applied quantitative and data roles.
- Ask each stand: Which teams hire quantitative researchers? What relevant openings exist and where are they based? What technical or domain requirements are essential? Who should receive a follow-up?
- After each conversation record the contact, role, location, requirements and agreed next step; follow up on the specific opportunity discussed.
